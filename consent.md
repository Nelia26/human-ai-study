You are invited to take part in a study of decision-making with and without
computer-generated advice. You will answer demographic questions and complete
15 short decision tasks. Some tasks ask for an independent answer first, followed
by a recommendation and an opportunity to revise your answer. You will also rate
confidence, trust in individual recommendations, and perceived autonomy.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time using “End participation”.
Stopping does not delete responses already submitted. There is no requirement
to follow a recommendation. These everyday scenarios may involve uncertainty;
recommendations are not guaranteed to be correct.

The study records your age, gender, education, field of study, AI experience,
choices, ratings, task order, and response times. It assigns a random participant
code and does not ask for your name or email address. Demographic combinations
can still be identifying. The hosting service may process connection logs.

Your private study link allows access to your unfinished session. Keep it private.
You may leave optional general-trust and field-of-study questions unanswered.
Contact the study team with questions before agreeing to participate.
